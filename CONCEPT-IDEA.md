## 1. One-sentence pitch

Help hiring leads and research partners judge Yorke's work for themselves: start with a problem they care about, try a demonstration, and see what he contributed.

## 2. The problem

A CV can say “supply chain resilience.” It cannot let a reader follow a material back to a mine and discover that two apparently independent supply paths rely on the same source. The reader has to find the right repository, work out whether anything runs, and distinguish Yorke's part from the team's. Conversely, a working demo rarely explains how it fits into his wider work. The brief identifies that gap; the time it costs a reader has not yet been measured.

## 3. Who it is for

| Reader | How they arrive | What they need to leave with |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hiring lead assessing technical judgment | LinkedIn bio or CV link | One decision Yorke helped make, the trade-off, and evidence they can share with a colleague. |
| Research or implementation partner | A shared project preview | A demo or paper in their domain, its limits, and who actually did the work. |
| Yorke and contributing maintainers | A project review | A way to correct credit, tags or an image once without rewriting the dashboard and both downloads. |

## 4. The idea

**Start with the question, not the technology.** A reader selects `supply-chain-resilience`, opens [Felementum's explorer](https://ethical-tech-colab.github.io/felementum/), and traces a dependency. Beside **Try the demo**, they see the question being tested, the credited contributors, and the [source's caveat](https://github.com/Ethical-Tech-CoLab/felementum): the supplier relationships are synthetic, not evidence about an actual company. They can return to the dashboard, combine topic and domain tags, group or sort by tag, or switch to newest work first. No matches should offer a clear way to reset the filters.

**What might sit side by side.** These examples come from public repositories reviewed on 2026-11-26. They are not a finished inventory or claims of sole authorship. Demo links are as published by their maintainers; verify availability in a browser before labeling a demo live.

| Reader's question | Evidence worth opening | What it establishes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What may an AI agent disclose, and who can authorize it? | [Wayfinder](https://github.com/yorkerhodes3/agentic-ontology) and [Sovereign Agent Trust Mesh](https://github.com/yorkerhodes3/sovereign-agent-trust-mesh) | Two different agentic workloads: personal disclosure rules and cross-organization authority using Ethereum registries. |
| When should a discounted ticket payment settle? | [Quest Payments](https://github.com/yorkerhodes3/quest-payments) | A commerce design comparing card capture, refunds and stablecoin escrow; label it **design**, not a live checkout. |

**How to read the body of work.** Give visitors four views of the *same* reviewed catalog:

| Lens | What the dashboard shows |
| --- | --- |
| Volume | Unique projects and runnable demos since **2026-01-01**, by period and source owner. A mirror or second deployment is not a second project. Show the inventory date. |
| Depth | For each project: problem, Yorke's role, a consequential choice, evidence and a known limit. Label the evidence available: design, working demonstration, evaluated result or documented field use. No inferred expertise score. |
| Type | Distinguish research/paper, decision model or simulation, tool/library, hardware or infrastructure, and application. A paper need not pretend to be an app. |
| Focus | Separate **methods** (applied AI usage, agentic workloads, frontier models, blockchain/Ethereum, decision models, edge hardware, open source) from **domains** (commerce, global affairs, supply chain resilience). Each summary links to the projects behind it; overlapping tag counts are not additive. |

**Coverage and upkeep.** Review both [yorkerhodes3](https://github.com/yorkerhodes3?tab=repositories) and [Ethical-Tech-CoLab](https://github.com/orgs/Ethical-Tech-CoLab/repositories) for work created **or substantively advanced** since the cutoff. A recent push is a discovery signal, not proof of contribution. Reconcile candidates against dated artifacts; record included, duplicate, excluded (with reason) or awaiting confirmation before calling the inventory complete. Distinguish built, co-built, directed and mentored; credit students and collaborators by their approved names and roles.

Each included project owns a small **`portfolio.json`** with a stable project ID, tags, kind, dated contribution and role, source/evidence links, optional demo, and credited image and icon references. Periodically reread those files into a dated dashboard snapshot rather than querying repositories on every visit. Report invalid or missing records for review; do not publish unreviewed claims or silently erase good records after a failed read. Honor withdrawals before the next publication.

**Visual identity and sharing.** Follow the [Wayfinder style guide](https://yorkerhodes3.github.io/agentic-ontology/style-guide.html): dark plum ground, lime actions, condensed Bebas Neue headings and Space Mono labels. Use contrast, visible keyboard focus, text labels and image descriptions so color is never the only cue. Audit each project's own page for a favicon and social preview. Preserve working assets; where missing, propose a project-specific icon and a 1200 × 630 share image with Open Graph and social-card metadata in a maintainer-reviewed change. Reuse a cleared screenshot or labeled diagram rather than inventing a product screen.

**Two downloads with different jobs.** *Download full portfolio (PDF)* always includes the whole published collection, regardless of filters: overview, every project narrative, credits, source/demo links and embedded project pictures. Keep the Wayfinder typography, color and layout in a paginated edition; pictures must remain visible offline. Interactive demos become captioned stills with links. *Download CV (PDF)* is a separate, text-selectable, single-column document: approved profile, reverse-chronological experience, education and selected work. Use Yorke's reviewed CV for career history, not GitHub guesses.

**Proposed line for LinkedIn, GitHub and the CV:**

> I make complex decisions testable, from what an AI agent can share to where a model helps predict outcomes, to where a 3rd party risk can break a company. 

Use the same line across the three bios, with a link to the portfolio where supported. Wayfinder and Felementum give readers a concrete way to challenge each half of the line; confirm the role on each before adoption.

## 5. What success looks like

| Signal | Proposed target and check |
| --- | --- |
| Discovery | Before launch, 4 of 5 trial readers find a relevant demo and correctly describe Yorke's role and one limitation within two minutes, starting from the bio link. |
| Honest coverage | At release review, every candidate from both accounts has a recorded disposition; every published project has approved credit, dated evidence, a tag file, a favicon and a share preview. |
| Maintainability | Before launch, a maintainer changes one project's tags. At the next refresh, its filters and focus totals change together; a malformed file is reported without breaking the catalog. |
| Portable evidence | At each release, open both downloads offline. The portfolio includes every published project and its cleared pictures without clipping; the CV has selectable text in reading order and matches the approved career record. |

## 6. Explicitly out of scope (for now)

No private or intentionally unlisted work without opt-in; no automatic edits to other people's repositories or bios. No hosting replicas of every demo, scoring people by commit volume, or interpreting humanitarian simulations as operational advice.

## 7. Constraints and realities

Prefer public static hosting with no account, visitor tracking or paid model call required to browse. Time and budget are not yet agreed. Image clearance, attribution and editorial review are likely to take more human time than publishing. Public visibility does not confer reuse rights: record source, license or permission and alt text; use an approved original illustration if a screenshot cannot travel in a PDF. Mark unavailable demos and offer source or a dated still instead.

Follow the [CoLab contribution practice](https://github.com/Ethical-Tech-CoLab/website/blob/HEAD/CONTRIBUTING.md): evidence for claims, focused pull requests and maintainer review, including for changes in source projects.

## 8. Alternatives considered

| Option | Trade-off |
| --- | --- |
| Keep LinkedIn, the CV and pinned repositories | Least upkeep, but a hiring lead still has to reconstruct the connection between a claim and a demo. |
| Use the CoLab website alone | Good home for the lab's work and team credit; not a record of personal projects or a personal CV. Link back rather than replace it. |
| Hand-curate one illustrated PDF | Easy to forward; difficult to explore by domain, and corrections diverge from the live catalog. |

## 9. Open questions for review

- Who can confirm Yorke's role on each CoLab project, and which related repositories should count as one project? Treat Yorke as Advisor/Prof when it is solely a student project. 
- Which CV edition is authoritative, and which project images are cleared for redistribution in both social previews and PDFs? All images are cleared for usage to enhance the description of a project. Find 3 good images, pick a default, allow navigation to the other 2 for each project. The default is the one that gets printed. 
- Who owns refresh and link review, at what cadence, and who handles corrections or takedowns? Yorke owns this and should be a function available in the repo for easy refresh. 

## 10. Assumptions

- **High risk:** Roles and image permissions can be verified for enough of the work to support the positioning. If not, narrow the published claims and disclose coverage rather than filling gaps.
- **Medium risk:** Upstream maintainers will accept the tag file and missing assets. Track pending changes separately; do not treat a local placeholder as upstream adoption.
- **Value test:** If readers can already find the same evidence and explain the same trade-offs as quickly from the CV alone, this site is not earning its maintenance cost.
